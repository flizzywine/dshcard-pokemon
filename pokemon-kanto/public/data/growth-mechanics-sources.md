# FRLG growth and move-rule sources

Rules here target the original English FireRed/LeafGreen release (revision 0), cross-checked against the upstream pret decompilation pinned to commit `ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982`.

- [pret/pokefirered at the pinned commit](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/tree/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982) is the original game reference. `src/data/pokemon/tmhm_learnsets.h` defines machine compatibility for each species; `include/constants/items.h` gives machine IDs and names; `src/data/pokemon/evolution.h` defines evolution triggers; `src/pokemon.c` contains level-up move and evolution handling; `src/battle_script_commands.c` implements EXP division, Exp. Share, and trainer bonuses.
- The vendored [Pokémon Showdown v0.11.11 FRLG learnset mod](https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown/tree/v0.11.11/data/mods/gen3frlg) supplies FRLG-specific level-up source entries (`3L`) and machine eligibility (`3M`). It is used through a separate `gen3frlg` Dex in `growth-mechanics.cjs`; the battle engine's generic `gen3` Dex is not treated as the FRLG learnset.
- `tmhm.json` contains the 50 TMs and seven HMs, with compatibility arrays derived from the pinned decompilation's bit table and move names resolved through the FRLG Dex. HM08 Dive is excluded because it is not an FRLG item.
- Gym prize TMs follow the pinned game's leader scripts and item constants: Brock TM39, Misty TM03, Lt. Surge TM34, Erika TM19, Koga TM06, Sabrina TM04, Blaine TM38, and Giovanni TM26. The corresponding `tmhm.json` rows supply the canonical moves and species compatibility.
- Field-item recipients and Oak aide thresholds follow the pinned map scripts and Pokédex checks, especially [`data/maps/Route2_EastBuilding/scripts.inc`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/data/maps/Route2_EastBuilding/scripts.inc) (HM05: 10 caught/owned) and [`data/maps/Route15_WestEntrance_2F/scripts.inc`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/data/maps/Route15_WestEntrance_2F/scripts.inc) (Exp. Share: 50 caught), plus [`src/prof_pc.c`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/src/prof_pc.c) and [`src/pokedex.c`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/src/pokedex.c), which establish the caught-count result slot. The script constant's name says “SEEN,” but its comparison uses the caught-count slot. Only these aide rewards are currently exposed because they have explicit Pokédex gates at represented map nodes.

## Implemented behavior

- Level thresholds use the species growth-rate curve. Each fainted foe rewards the surviving Pokémon recorded as having entered against that foe, plus living Exp. Share holders. Base EXP is floored before division and the trainer bonus is applied after each share is floored; level-100 Pokémon are excluded from rewards, while living level-100 participants and Exp. Share holders remain in the original division counts. EVs go only to living, below-level-100 participants and holders. Rewards remain subject to the journey's explicit Gym level caps. Old battle records without per-foe participation data conservatively reuse their surviving active participants for each defeated foe.
- Every intervening level is processed in order for level-up moves and friendship. FRLG defers battle evolutions until after battle, so a single reward spanning several levels checks evolution once from the original species at the resulting level; moves beyond four are queued in `待学招式` until accepted or declined. Level-up friendship uses FRLG's current-happiness deltas (+5 below 100, +3 below 200, otherwise +2).
- Stone evolutions are directly usable through `evolvePokemon`; friendship-triggered evolutions happen on level gain at friendship 220. Trade evolutions are rejected because this single-player package has no trading partner. FRLG's time-based Eevee evolutions are not offered.
- TMs are consumed after a valid move replacement; HMs remain reusable. HM moves require the Move Deleter action in Fuchsia City to forget and cannot be replaced with an ordinary TM.
- A first victory at each Gym grants its original one-use TM prize. HM05 and Exp. Share keep the original 10-caught/owned and 50-caught-species Pokédex thresholds, and each aide claim is recorded once in the MVU. HM01–HM04 are exposed through their S.S. Anne captain, Route 16 house, Safari Secret House, and Warden/Gold Teeth story events. HM06/HM07 are not awarded because their Ember Spa / Icefall Cave locations are on the omitted Sevii Islands.
- Smoke Ball is represented as a holdable Gen 3 item even though the vendored Showdown item dictionary omits it; its wild-battle escape behavior is handled by the battle adapter. Full Heal cures only the six major status conditions, matching FRLG.
- Outside-battle machine use, healing items, and stone evolution mutate a cloned MVU and validate before returning, so a rejected operation does not spend an item or partially change a Pokémon.

## Not modeled

- The package does not reproduce every friendship gain/loss event, walking counter, soothing-bell modifier, luxury-ball modifier, or capture-location modifier. The broad Route 2 and Route 15 nodes stand in for their specific aide buildings. HM06/HM07 remain unavailable because their original locations and required story/key-item flags are on the omitted Sevii Islands.
- A literal link-cable trade cannot be simulated. Held-item trade evolutions and time-of-day-based evolutions are therefore not exposed as local operations.
- Lucky Egg EXP bonuses now use per-defeated-foe held-item snapshots and the original integer multiplication order. Traded-Pokémon bonuses, link-battle exclusions, and other multiplayer-only EXP exceptions remain outside this single-player package. Participant tracking uses the battle simulator's active-turn record.
