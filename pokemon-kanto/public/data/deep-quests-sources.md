# Deep commissions and permanent journey mechanisms

`deep-quests.json` contains 33 original, multi-stage side stories for the Kanto journey. Every one of the eleven towns offers at least two; seven additional commissions begin at named wild-route or cave locations where their existing trainer characters are present. Each arc combines at least three kinds of stored activity, such as catching a specified species or type, discovering new Pokédex entries, recorded care, bond changes, route visits, badges, and League results. Stage completion is calculated from MVU baselines and event records; narrative text cannot complete a stage or grant a mechanism. Several arcs require an earlier tool before offering its upgrade, so the follow-up stories extend a chain of useful rewards instead of being independent archive unlocks.

The game-mechanics comparison elsewhere in this project uses `pret/pokefirered` at fixed commit `ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982`; this file describes authored optional content, not a cartridge-mechanics claim. Character roles and Kanto geography follow the project's bundled FRLG map and character catalog. Quest dialogue, dilemmas, prices, choices, endings, and rewards are newly written and are not cartridge dialogue or canonical events. Existing named trainer characters serve as the commission-givers at the six wild locations; no new NPC IDs or reward mechanisms were introduced.

Permanent mechanisms are stored separately under `world.机制奖励.已解锁` and are awarded exactly once by the explicit final hand-in action. `quest-gadgets.cjs` provides `ensure`, `unlock`, `view`, `modifiers`, and `applyConfig`. The experience-sharing device starts in `off`, which keeps original participant and held Exp. Share behavior. `split` and `journey` are project-specific modes and are unavailable until the Oak story unlocks the device. The legal move terminal only advertises species-compatible TM moves and level-up moves; the free-TM research permit applies only to an already-owned compatible TM used through the original terminal interface. Direct bag use still consumes that machine. Item bundles are validated against the project's medicine and TM catalogs, checked as a whole against the 999-item cap, and delivered only at final claim; a failed capacity check leaves the commission ready and the bag unchanged.

Several commissions now foreground consent, field safety, and accountability through authored character choices: Ash's route notes record a partner's chosen rest, Erika's garden route protects quiet habitat, the Rocket defector must verify a delivery record without being granted absolution, Blaine signs an audit trail for earlier lab failures, and Blue's league archive credits other challengers. Koga's camp exercise begins at the Safari Zone field site and requires the existing Erika commission to unlock `campKit`; one actual campsite action advances the camp stage, while the following care record can be made through the supported camp-rest action. It adds no extra bond grind. These dilemmas and character moments are original adaptations, not scenes or quotations from the source works. Camp rest also remains a valid route to the existing bond thresholds because progress uses saved trust changes.

Short primary-source anchors for broad character and story context: Pokémon's official [Season 1 episode guide](https://www.pokemon.com/uk/animation/seasons/1) and its [Bulbasaur and the Hidden Village synopsis](https://www.pokemon.com/uk/animation/seasons/1/episode-10-bulbasaur-and-the-hidden-village) for the Ash/Misty/Brock travel group and care for vulnerable Pokémon; Shogakukan's [Pokémon Adventures volume 1 synopsis](https://e-comi.shogakukan.co.jp/books/091493310000d0000000) for Red, Green, the Pokédex journey, and Team Rocket. They calibrate roles and themes only; no episode or manga dialogue is reused.

The reward effects and bundle rewards are local game rules, not FireRed/LeafGreen cartridge items or effects. Bundle contents use items already recognized by the MVU bag, including compatible TM machines, PP Up/Max, recovery supplies, Rare Candy, and useful capture balls. Master Ball is intentionally omitted from these authored reward bundles to preserve capture progression; special-ball rules are supported separately. Story evidence and rewards remain in MVU state so reloading does not repeat a stage or hand-in.
