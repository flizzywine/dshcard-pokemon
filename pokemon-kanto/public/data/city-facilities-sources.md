# Kanto city facilities source notes

Facilities use the pinned `pret/pokefirered` revision `ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982`. Direct links are also recorded in `city-facilities.json`.

## Celadon Game Corner

- The Coin Case is an in-game key item obtained from a visitor inside the Game Corner. This implementation grants it once through `facility-coin-case` and stores one key-item record.
- The original coin purchase menu offers 50 coins for ¥1,000 or 500 coins for ¥10,000. The implementation uses those exact prices and prevents the 9,999 coin wallet limit from being exceeded.
- The prize-room script checks for the Coin Case. The implementation offers the four supported held-item prizes (Smoke Ball 800, Miracle Seed 1,000, Charcoal 1,000, Mystic Water 1,000), plus TM13 Ice Beam (4,000), TM23 Iron Tail (3,500), TM24 Thunderbolt (4,000), TM30 Shadow Ball (4,500), and TM35 Flamethrower (4,000).
- The same script offers version-specific prize Pokémon and levels: FireRed Abra (180, Lv. 9), Clefairy (500, Lv. 8), Dratini (2,800, Lv. 18), Scyther (5,500, Lv. 25), and Porygon (9,999, Lv. 26); LeafGreen Abra (120, Lv. 7), Clefairy (750, Lv. 12), Pinsir (2,500, Lv. 18), Dratini (4,600, Lv. 24), and Porygon (6,500, Lv. 18). Redemption creates an owned Pokémon with no wild-capture rewards or held item, records it in the owned dex, and deposits to the PC when the party is full. A full party and PC prevents redemption and coin deduction.
- Yellow Flute is available for 1,600 coins and is a reusable confusion-curing battle item. Slot-machine play remains disabled; no approximate odds are presented as original game behavior.

Primary sources: [`CeladonCity_GameCorner/scripts.inc`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/data/maps/CeladonCity_GameCorner/scripts.inc), [`CeladonCity_GameCorner_PrizeRoom/scripts.inc`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/data/maps/CeladonCity_GameCorner_PrizeRoom/scripts.inc). ## Celadon Department Store

- The store is represented as a floor selector and focused service menu, not a walkable multi-floor map. Available service floors are 2F, 4F, 5F and the rooftop; 1F is a lobby and 3F's counter tutoring is not simulated.
- 2F sells the supported catalog subset: Great Ball, Super Potion, Revive and five status cures. Super Repel is omitted because its item row is absent; the floor's TM rack is omitted because local TM data has no purchase prices. 4F sells the four evolution stones and Poké Doll; Retro Mail is omitted because it has no inventory row. 5F sells the supported X battle items; vitamins are omitted because their inventory rows are not present.
- The rooftop vending machine sells Fresh Water for ¥200, Soda Pop for ¥300, and Lemonade for ¥350. These drinks use the MVU's shared bag inventory. The girl trades Fresh Water for TM16 (Light Screen), Soda Pop for TM20 (Safeguard), and Lemonade for TM33 (Reflect), each once. A trade checks reward inventory space before consuming the drink.

Primary sources: [`CeladonCity_DepartmentStore_1F/scripts.inc`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/data/maps/CeladonCity_DepartmentStore_1F/scripts.inc), [`CeladonCity_DepartmentStore_2F/scripts.inc`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/data/maps/CeladonCity_DepartmentStore_2F/scripts.inc), [`CeladonCity_DepartmentStore_4F/scripts.inc`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/data/maps/CeladonCity_DepartmentStore_4F/scripts.inc), [`CeladonCity_DepartmentStore_5F/scripts.inc`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/data/maps/CeladonCity_DepartmentStore_5F/scripts.inc), and [`CeladonCity_DepartmentStore_Roof/scripts.inc`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/data/maps/CeladonCity_DepartmentStore_Roof/scripts.inc).

## Safari Zone

- The source entry script charges ¥500; `EnterSafariMode` begins with 30 Safari Balls and 600 steps (`src/safari_zone.c`).
- Species and encounter levels are read from the checked-in `frlg-encounters.json` Safari pools. The source encounter rates and weighted slots are retained. `speciesMechanics` stores source `catchRate` and `safariZoneFleeRate` for every species in those version pools, transcribed from `src/data/pokemon/species_info.h` at the pinned revision.
- Safari starts catch factor at `catchRate * 100 / 1275` (integer division) and escape factor at `max(2, safariZoneFleeRate * 100 / 1275)`. A Safari Ball uses multiplier 15. Because the module's generated Safari target is at full HP with no status, it applies the source catch odds formula at full HP and integer `captureThreshold`, then performs up to four 16-bit RNG checks; the first three passed checks correspond to visible shakes, while failure of the fourth still displays three shakes. It does not use the prior 40% placeholder.
- Bait adds a source-style randomized 2–6 turn timer (capped at 6), clears the rock timer, halves catch factor with minimum 3, and uses flee chance `max(1, floor(escapeFactor / 4)) * 5%`. Rock adds a randomized 2–6 turn timer (capped at 6), clears bait, doubles catch factor (capped at 20), and uses flee chance `min(20, escapeFactor * 2) * 5%`. Otherwise the flee chance is `escapeFactor * 5%`. The species flees when a deterministic roll in `[0,99]` is below that percentage. A surviving target's watch action decrements its active timer; source watch handling resets catch factor to baseline only when the rock timer expires. Bait expiry leaves the reduced catch factor in place.
- Distinct limits: Safari Balls are consumed on throws, and moving subtracts the requested steps, using the extracted source encounter rate for each step. `Run` flees only the current target; `exit` ends the paid visit. The action module exposes the target and its current factors/timers in `view()`.

Primary mechanics sources: [`data/maps/FuchsiaCity_SafariZone_Entrance/scripts.inc`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/data/maps/FuchsiaCity_SafariZone_Entrance/scripts.inc), [`src/safari_zone.c`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/src/safari_zone.c), [`src/data/wild_encounters.json`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/src/data/wild_encounters.json), [`src/data/pokemon/species_info.h`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/src/data/pokemon/species_info.h), [`src/battle_main.c`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/src/battle_main.c), [`src/battle_script_commands.c`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/src/battle_script_commands.c), and [`src/battle_ai_script_commands.c`](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/blob/ce6406d8c2eaf46d543c8a2606de0c3cb1951982/src/battle_ai_script_commands.c). The extracted version pools, levels, weights, and encounter rates are in `frlg-encounters.json`.

## Town entries

The other town entries are discoverable facility links and descriptive venue labels only. They do not create items, story flags, access rights, or battles; those effects remain with the existing quest, campaign, and battle rules.
